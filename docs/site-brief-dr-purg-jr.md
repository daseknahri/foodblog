# Dr Purg Jr. Site Brief

## Launch Identity

- Domain: `https://health.ibnbatoutaweb.com`
- Brand: `Dr Purg Jr.`
- Language: English, `en_US`
- Admin locale: English, `en_US`, forced through `content/site-profile.json`
- Audience country: United States
- Niche: shocking health facts, body-signal explainers, food and habit myths, sleep and energy context

## Positioning

Dr Purg Jr. is a Facebook/mobile-first health-facts publication. The site can use curiosity-led titles, but the article body must stay careful: explain the possible context, avoid panic, and remind readers that a general article is not personal medical advice.

The launch pack is built as 20 original article posts. The engine still uses the historical `recipes` archive slug for compatibility, but the visible page title and copy present it as `Health Facts`.

## Content Guardrails

- Do not diagnose readers.
- Do not promise cures, personal outcomes, fast fixes, or guaranteed results.
- Do not use fear-only health clickbait.
- Do not recommend medication, supplements, tests, or treatment changes as a general instruction.
- Do encourage professional care for severe, sudden, persistent, worsening, or personal symptoms.
- Keep Facebook hooks curiosity-based, then pay them off with calm context.

## Monetization Strategy

This launch is not AdSense-first.

- Keep `ADSENSE_ENABLE=0`.
- Keep `ADSENSE_CLIENT_ID=` and `ADSENSE_PUB_ID=` blank.
- Keep `DISPLAY_ADS_ENABLE=0`, `MONETAG_ENABLE=0`, `GA_ENABLE=0`, and `HISTATS_ENABLE=0` in source defaults.
- Add instant/native network snippets only in Coolify, base64 encoded, one placement at a time.
- Keep ads off legal, policy, contact, search, feed, 404, and admin views.
- Start with baseline mode and single-post-only placements.

## Launch Content Pack

The first pack covers:

- sleep and phone-light facts;
- dry mouth, cold hands, headaches, standing dizziness, and other body-signal explainers;
- caffeine timing, hydration, stress, posture, walking after meals, and sleep debt;
- sugar labels, hidden salt, packaged-food cravings, fiber, and breakfast myths;
- a safety-focused guide on warning signs readers should not scroll past.

Every launch post has:

- article kind;
- guide category assignment for compatibility with existing validation;
- 5 substantive sections;
- 4 takeaways;
- 3 FAQ items;
- 4 internal related links;
- SEO title and meta description;
- a matching generated PNG image and image-plan entry.
